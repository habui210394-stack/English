ĐÁP ÁN – Bộ đề like, favourite, have to / has to, do / does

Em hãy làm bài xong rồi mới xem đáp án nhé!

Part 1. Reorder the words

  1. I like apples.
  2. She likes cats.
  3. Do you like dogs?
  4. He does not like milk.
  5. We like playing football.
  6. My favourite colour is blue.
  7. What is your favourite food?
  8. Her favourite subject is Maths.
  9. His favourite animal is the dog.
  10. I have to do my homework.
  11. He has to brush his teeth.
  12. She has to clean her room.
  13. We have to listen to the teacher.
  14. Tom has to get up early.
  15. You have to wash your hands.

  1. My sister likes pink.  →  B
  2. They go to school every day.  →  B
  3. He watches TV in the evening.  →  C
  4. I like playing football.  →  C
  5. Do you like fish?  →  A
  6. Does she like milk?  →  B
  7. She washes her face every morning.  →  C
  8. Tom has to do his homework.  →  B
  9. We have to keep the classroom clean.  →  B
  10. My dog doesn't like bananas.  →  B
  11. Mai likes drawing pictures.  →  C
  12. My brother plays football on Sundays.  →  B
  13. Do you have to wear a uniform?  →  B

Part 3. True / False

Hội thoại 1

  1. Lan's favourite colour is pink.  →  T
  2. Tom's favourite colour is red.  →  F (Tom thích màu xanh dương (blue).)
  3. Tom likes dogs.  →  T
  4. Lan likes dogs.  →  F (Lan thích mèo (cats).)
  5. Tom has a dog.  →  T

Hội thoại 2

  1. Mai can play with Nam now.  →  F (Mai không chơi được vì phải dọn phòng.)
  2. Mai has to clean her room.  →  T
  3. Mai has to do her English homework.  →  T
  4. Mai's brother has to wash the dishes.  →  T
  5. Nam and Mai will play after lunch.  →  F (Hai bạn chơi sau bữa tối (after dinner).)

Hội thoại 3

  1. Lucy likes bread and milk.  →  T
  2. Lucy's favourite food is noodles.  →  F (Món yêu thích của Lucy là bánh mì (bread).)
  3. Ben's favourite food is noodles.  →  T
  4. Ben's sister likes noodles.  →  F (Em gái Ben không thích mì (doesn't like).)
  5. Ben's sister has an egg every morning.  →  T
