Ghi nhớ nhanh:
  • I / You / We / They + like → I like cats.   He / She / It / tên riêng + likes → She likes cats.
  • Sau like có thể dùng V-ing: I like playing football.
  • Do / Does đặt đầu câu hỏi: Do you like fish? – Does she like fish? Sau do / does dùng động từ nguyên mẫu (không thêm s).
  • Phủ định: I do not (don't) like… / She does not (doesn't) like…
  • favourite = yêu thích nhất: My favourite colour is blue.
  • have to / has to = phải làm gì: I / You / We / They have to …; He / She / Tom has to …
  • Động từ thêm es sau: -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o (watches, washes, goes). Động từ tận cùng phụ âm + y đổi y → ies (studies).

Hội thoại 1

Lan: My favourite colour is pink. What about you?

Tom: I like blue. It is my favourite colour.

Lan: Do you like animals?

Tom: Yes, I do. I like dogs. I have a dog.

Lan: I like cats. I don't have a dog.

2. Tom's favourite colour is red.   ____
3. Tom likes dogs.   ____
